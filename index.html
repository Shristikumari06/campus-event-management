ABOUT CAMPUSCONNECT

Bringing Campus Life and Community Together

CampusConnect was created with a clear mission: eliminate the chaos of scattered WhatsApp groups, torn bulletin board flyers, and missed registration deadlines.

We provide a single, unified digital destination where students can effortlessly discover what is happening across the university, RSVP in seconds, and showcase their talents. From 36-hour hackathons to electrifying cultural concerts and varsity sports tournaments, CampusConnect empowers both organizers and attendees.
